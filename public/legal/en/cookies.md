# Cookie Policy

This policy explains how REDSOUTH Studio uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

## 1. What are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide a better user experience.

## 2. How We Use Cookies
**We only use Strictly Necessary and Functional cookies.** 
We do not use advertising, marketing, or invasive third-party tracking cookies. The local data we store is used exclusively to make the web app function correctly:

- **Authentication:** We use local storage (like the PocketBase auth token) to keep you securely logged in across sessions.
- **Security:** We generate a unique device identifier (`rs_device_id`) to manage your active sessions and allow you to remotely log out of other devices.
- **Preferences:** To remember your language selection (English/Spanish) and UI theme (Light/Dark).

## 3. Third-Party Services
We do not inject third-party trackers. However, our website may load functional assets from external sources (such as Google Fonts or OAuth login providers like GitHub/Discord). These providers may receive standard network information (like your IP address) as part of delivering those assets, but we do not allow them to place tracking cookies on our site.

## 4. Managing Cookies
Because our cookies are strictly necessary for the core functionality of the website (like logging in), you cannot disable them through our site. If you wish to block all cookies, you can do so through your browser settings, but please note that REDSOUTH Studio will not function correctly without them.
